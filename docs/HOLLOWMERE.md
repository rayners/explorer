# Hollowmere — Beyond the Last Lantern

Play: https://rayners.github.io/explorer/hollowmere/

A dark fantasy exploration game about a company of the living, crossing a forest that keeps its own accounts. It is a separate game built on Far Wake's engine. Its saves, preferences and tab ownership are independent.

Begin at **Mournwatch**. Accept **Beyond the last lantern**, take the Old Road, and **Read the signs**. Return to a refuge and report the completed oath. Mapping opens more places and paths: the settlements of the Last Hearth, Ashenwood, and the underground sanctuaries and tombs of the Saints Below.

## Party composition

The initial company includes Bran Ash (fighter), Ves Rook (mage), and Sister Elin (cleric). Four places are available initially, expandable to eight through Company tents and a Retinue charter. Recruit additional fighters, mages, clerics and scouts in Refuge; new companions begin resting.

- **Fighters:** a fighter must join Expedition to lead site exploration. They also improve armed combat and confrontation with the White Guest.
- **Mages:** improve mapping, are required to unravel wards, and contribute to combat. Mages on Expedition can track an internal threat.
- **Clerics:** on Support, they enable Prayer of mending and may save a companion from a fatal curse attack. On Expedition, they can consecrate shrines. Moving a cleric between teams creates a real tradeoff.
- **Scouts:** improve gathering on Expedition and help threat tracking on Support. They also make selected support tasks faster.

Assignments lock while the affected team is working. Idle and resting companions can join another idle team. Equipment condition, supplies, and capacity matter alongside classes. Injured members remain usable at reduced effectiveness. Refuge treatment and cleric support heal survivors; they do not resurrect the dead.

## The White Guest

Opening the profaned reliquary in the Unshriven Crypt is optional and explicitly warns of permanent losses. Something may follow the company afterward. After two completed activities, it reveals itself and consequential time pauses. Search for it, bind it, or confront it once located. Repeated failures can lead to a fatal attack during an explicit response. A support cleric can sometimes save the victim.

**Burn the cursed stores** is always a safe way to end the incident: lose 20% of physical supplies with no casualties. Crowns are retained. Successful containment lets ordinary work resume, but the Guest remains until banished or purged. No threat response or casualty is resolved while you are away.

## Recovery and saves

If stranded, request an escort from Journey. Its 100-crown debt is repaid through refuge work. If everyone has died, a free militia volunteer is available at a refuge. Assign the volunteer to Expedition, work for crowns, and rebuild the company.

Settings supports JSON export, download, backup retrieval and import. Far Wake saves are rejected rather than converted. Both games use one offline service worker, but each has its own install manifest and starting page. Installation requires HTTPS.

This is an authored first region of play with seeded detail variation, not an endless procedural dungeon or roguelite progression system. Those remain later extensions.
