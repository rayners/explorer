# Decisions and rationale

Recorded against v0.2.1 on 2026-10-27; updated for v0.4.0 on 2026-10-28. These are implemented choices unless marked deferred. User goals are separate in [REQUIREMENTS.md](REQUIREMENTS.md).

| Decision                                                                   | Reason and consequence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Static Vite/TypeScript application with hand-written DOM                   | Fits GitHub Pages and browser-local play without accounts or a server. Runtime storage is device/browser-specific; export/import is the transfer mechanism.                                                                                                                             |
| Shared deterministic engine, separate game definitions and themed mounts   | Makes a second setting practical without duplicating mechanics. Skills/resources are IDs and catalogs; engine code does not branch on science-fiction or fantasy names.                                                                                                                 |
| Original code-generated pixel art and local assets                         | Keeps the intended visual style, small offline payload and independent themes without runtime asset services. Film inspiration supplies tone, not franchise characters or artwork.                                                                                                      |
| Authored topology with seeded detail variation                             | Makes first goals and recovery paths testable. Seeds currently vary frontier details; they do not generate entire campaigns. Full procedural generation is deferred.                                                                                                                    |
| One primary job plus one support job                                       | Offers crew/logistics choices without a large task queue or constant tapping. Orders finish once and do not automatically repeat.                                                                                                                                                       |
| Separate real wait and fictional calendar                                  | A long interstellar journey can take minutes of play while advancing years in the setting. This is logistics fiction, not orbital or relativistic simulation. Accepted work does not expire during cryosleep.                                                                           |
| Time advances through completion boundaries                                | Costs are paid at dispatch and rewards delivered once. Saved PRNG state avoids frame-rate and reload-dependent outcomes. Catch-up is capped at 24 hours and pauses at decisions.                                                                                                        |
| Far Wake interregional travel uses region-wide connections                 | Open space does not require departure from a particular planet. Include local transfers in duration and cost. The generic default remains location endpoints for roads, passes or gates. Route plans preview known itineraries; each leg still needs an order.                          |
| Recovery instead of routine irreversible failure                           | Travel hazards injure; surrender provides a resource-free encounter exit. Recovery transport creates work-repayable debt. A free emergency recruit is available at a hub after total crew loss.                                                                                         |
| Recruitment and generic activity requirements                              | Roles matter through skills, assignment and required headcount. Four accommodation places expand to six and eight. New hires rest until assigned. Dead members retain memorial records and free their berths.                                                                           |
| Internal threat with explicit response warnings                            | Supports surprise or authored stowaway horror and authored fantasy curses without punishing absence. Hidden incidents develop at completion boundaries; revealed alerts pause consequential time. Only explicit threat responses can kill crew.                                         |
| Guaranteed purge with physical-cargo loss                                  | Gives every incident a reliable escape, including no-resource/no-survivor states. It loses 20% of physical cargo, retaining currency. Containment permits operations but does not claim a kill.                                                                                         |
| Preserve committed jobs through casualties                                 | A response may change available personnel, but must not erase the paid duration/cost commitment of a journey already underway. New orders still validate current staffing.                                                                                                              |
| Initial faction layer: reputation, licenses, relief, prices and encounters | Supplies political and armed choices at manageable scope. Autonomous fleets, territory and diplomacy simulations remain future possibilities.                                                                                                                                           |
| Independent game namespaces with one shared browser host                   | Reuses lifecycle, storage and coordination fixes while allowing both games to run and save independently. Cross-game imports are rejected.                                                                                                                                              |
| Backup/write replacement before swapping memory                            | Import/reset write failure must leave the active expedition intact. Preserve unreadable originals where storage permits; keep export available and errors visible.                                                                                                                      |
| Web Locks plus storage-lease fallback                                      | Secure contexts support atomic command ownership. HTTP LAN previews use best-effort renewable leases; they do not provide the same guarantee. Secondary tabs display saved state.                                                                                                       |
| Separate save-envelope, state-schema and generation versions               | Envelope stays 1; state is 3 with migration from 1 and 2; generation 1 preserves authored names and generation 2 uses seeded names. Reject unsupported worlds instead of silently recreating different content for the same seed. Checksums detect accidental corruption, not cheating. |
| Root library, separate game entry pages                                    | Mirrors clicker's game-selection pattern. The library only recognizes save metadata for Play/Resume; it never simulates or changes a checkpoint. Only implemented games get cards.                                                                                                      |
| Preserve Far Wake's historical installation identity                       | Keep the root manifest URL and an explicit ID equal to its previous root start URL under the deployment base. New start/scope point to `far-wake/`; legacy standalone root launches redirect there. `?games=1` explicitly opens the library.                                            |
| One root service worker, separate game manifests                           | All pages can be precached and visited offline, including from a picker-first visit. Each game installs with its own starting page. Real iPhone install/upgrade acceptance is still pending.                                                                                            |
| Automated WebKit coverage plus explicit physical acceptance                | Actual WebKit with iPhone/iPad settings catches browser behavior; it cannot prove hardware Safari, suspension or home-screen upgrade behavior. Never relabel pending manual checks as passed.                                                                                           |

## Alternatives and limits

The original Far Wake implementation used direct route endpoints, then exposed departure-world guidance. User feedback led to region-wide space departures instead; guidance alone did not satisfy the intended fiction. The engine retains both policies for other settings.

Hollowmere reuses generic mechanisms but has a separate themed UI, rather than a complete skin/configuration language. It currently imports shared UI styles. A data-only game authoring system is not implemented or required.

No roguelite loss/reset economy has been selected. Permanent unlocks, procedural mission construction, generator persistence and alternative ships need designs before implementation. See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

## Later direction: surprise onset

The user clarified on 2026-10-27 that a creature should sometimes be a surprise. The v0.3 implementation adds silent exposure from ordinary site exploration alongside the explicit specimen trigger. The user approved this plus a political discovery follow-up on 2026-10-28; explicit response warnings, no offline casualties and guaranteed recovery remain. [EVENTS.md](EVENTS.md) records the implemented selection rules and settlement claim. The claim waits for docking without stopping time; committing a recipient applies one-time payment and persistent faction standing. A durable ledger prevents repeat rewards. Larger event catalogs and a procedural galaxy remain deferred.

## Seeded names (v0.4)

Both games now generate crew, faction, region and location names from themed vocabularies using pure functions. Names have independent seeded domains and never consume combat/event RNG. Stable IDs and geography remain unchanged. Factories accept a saved generation version: 1 reproduces existing authored names/recruits; 2 is the default for fresh expeditions. No state-schema migration is needed because seed/version and crew names already persist. Vocabulary changes require a new compatible generation version. References in narrative resolve by identity after naming. See [NAMES.md](NAMES.md).
